Knight & Luna, under the bar

The Knight & Luna emblem you liked, with the barbell from Earn the Armor worked in. He lifts, she spots, and the whole thing still feels like the two of them.

Idea

Same knight, same Luna, but now he's under the bar. The emblem swaps the shield for a loaded barbell across his shoulders, and Luna sits next to him like she's spotting. The halo and the stars stay. The ring reads SHUG KNIGHT on top and WITH LUNA · EARN THE ARMOR along the bottom, with little dumbbells as the stops.

The system

Emblem for big spots: the site footer, the back of a tee, a patch. Crest for avatars and headers: the round 4 shield with Luna's face, where the helm's eye slit is now the barbell from Earn the Armor, plates poking out past the cheeks. Favicon is that crowned helm alone, since it stays crisp at 16px. Sir Luna is back as the app icon, chin resting on a bar.

Type and colour

Young Serif for the name, same as before. The tagline is Big Shoulders Bold, slanted, which is where the gym energy comes in. Under the name sits a thin barbell rule with red plates on each end. Same palette as round 4; the plates borrow the heraldic red and the bar borrows the crown gold, so nothing new had to be added.

Plain

The name and a woven SS signet. Nothing else on the line. For resumes, LinkedIn and client work.

Idea

Round 4 kept, with the descriptor line removed. The lockup is now just the signet, a thin cobalt rule and the name. With one line of type, the signet can be larger and carry more of the personality.

Type

Instrument Serif, upright, for the name, set slightly tight. The signet uses the italic cut of the same family, so the pair reads as one voice: a formal name and a looser, more personal initial.

Signet

Two italic S shapes, woven. The second S passes over the first at the spine and tucks behind its lower curve, like two links of a chain. The cuts are real gaps, so it works on any ground and in one colour. The favicon keeps one S and the cobalt full stop.
